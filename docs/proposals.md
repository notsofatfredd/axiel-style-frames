# PROPOSED decisions (G6 technical prototype, G7 assets)

Choices the build made where the protocol is silent or two sources disagree. Each needs the Creative Director's OK
(or a change) before G6 is signed. Earlier gates list theirs in the README (G2 PROPOSED, G4 changed keys, G5 timing).

| # | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| P6-1 | **Rain falls toward the façades (moves in −z).** | §3.2 says "a 12° slant toward the +z façades". SF-06 drew its frozen streaks leaning toward the camera instead. The prototype follows §3.2; the frame and the prototype disagree until the CD picks one. | `site/components/scenes/rain.ts` (DIR) |
| P6-2 | **Paper grain is a DOM overlay** (fixed full-screen tile, overlay blend, 2%), not a post-processing pass. | Same fibre texture and strength as the frames (js/app.js GrainShader), one less full-screen pass on Mid, and it never touches the WebGL budget. @react-three/postprocessing is not installed. | `site/components/overlay/Grain.tsx` |
| P6-3 | **Freeze labels only inside 0.68 to 0.80** (the labels row), and only once AMOS is built. | §5.3 says "on freeze" without a window; outside the rain there are no drops to hang them on. | `site/components/Freeze.tsx` |
| P6-4 | **Labels type back out at 15 ms per char** when scrolling resumes (in at 40 ms per char, §5.3). | The protocol gives the type-in only. A quick un-type reads as the reading being released, not a cut. | `site/components/overlay/DropLabels.tsx` |
| P6-5 | **A label flips to the inboard side** of its drop when the outboard side has no room. | Keeps every reading whole on screen at 390 px wide. | `site/components/scenes/amosWorld.ts` (pickLabels) |
| P6-6 | **Registration order** of the repaired building as the beads row scrubs: window 0.15, nameplate 0.35, fascia 0.50, door 0.60, stain 0.80, other 0.95 (G channel of the integrity mask). | §5.3 says "per element" without an order. Window first because it is the fix; the stain last so the fault reads after the work is shown passing. | `site/components/scenes/integrity.ts` |
| P6-7 | **Rain targets:** 25% of drops aimed at the façade mask, 40% the street, 35% the plateau roofs. | Enough drops hit the building that beading and staining read; the rest keep the street wet. | `site/components/scenes/rain.ts` |
| P6-8 | **Cloud base at y 26**, drops visible 0.8 s after impact (bead roll or soak). | Above every roof in frame at K14 to K16; 0.8 s lets a bead visibly roll. | `site/components/scenes/rain.ts` (TOP, POST) |
| P6-9 | **Mid tier water film** uses an environment tint instead of a mirror. | §10.1 says Mid has no planar reflection; the film still reads as wet. | `site/shaders/waterFilm.glsl` (NO_REFLECT) |
| P6-10 | **Phone layout below 760 px wide** (phone camera keys, 900vh scroll, phone copy sizes). | The frames and animatic use 390 px; 760 covers large phones in portrait without catching tablets. | `site/lib/tiers.ts` |
| P6-11 | **fps bench rule:** scripted descent 0.58 to 0.79, 3 s hold (frozen), on to 0.82 and back to 0.58 at 40 s per unit of p (30 on phone); passes if the mean is at least 97% of the target and the 1% low at least 75%. | §6 gives the targets (60 desktop, 45 mobile) but not how to measure them. | `site/components/overlay/Bench.tsx`, `?bench=1` |
| P6-12 | **Fonts load from Google Fonts by family name**, not next/font. | The canvas label and copy textures in the shared kits name 'Montserrat' and 'JetBrains Mono' directly; next/font renames the families. | `site/app/layout.tsx` |
| P6-13 | **Review hosting:** the prototype is served from the existing GitHub Pages site at `/proto/`, noindex, for review only. | Not the production host: hosting is a human decision at G6 (README). | `.github/workflows/render.yml` |

## G7 assets

| # | Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| P7-1 | **The bake is ambient occlusion only**, not full lighting: contact darkening on TEXCOORD_1 (city ground 2048×1024 at 6 m reach; strata cliff 1024×1024 and floor 1024×256 at 3 m), multiplied in under each scene's own light. | §7.1 says "bake lighting where static". The geometry is static but the light is not: the city is relit for dusk, rain and the lit windows, the strata for the gold seam. A full light bake would be right in one scene and wrong in the rest. | `tools/bake.py`, `assets.html` (ASSETS lightmaps) |
| P7-2 | **The kit materials ride inside the GLB** as a recipe in `extras.axiel` (shader parameters, uniforms), and the site rebuilds them on load; the glTF material is a plain PBR fallback. Uniform textures (carve masks, inlay masks, symbol heights) ship as KTX2 sidecars named `<asset>.<uniform>.ktx2`. | The procedural detail (world-space grain, carving, façade windows, wax relief) is shader code and cannot be written as glTF. One source (the kits) for the frames, the prototype and the GLBs means a GLB cannot drift from the signed frames. | `js/kit/glb.js` |
| P7-3 | **Variant names for tier and state:** `city_fill-high_v1`, `city_fill-mid_v1`, `amos_faultbuilding-empty_v1`, `amos_faultbuilding-repaired_v1`. | §7.1 gives `scene_object_variant.glb` with one variant slot; tier and state both need it, so they share it with a hyphen. The city is shared by several scenes, so its scene slot is `city`. | `assets.html` (ASSETS) |
| P7-4 | **The strata cliff and floor ship as a GLB**, although §7 lists the cliff as code-native. | The AO bake needs fixed UVs and fixed geometry to land on. The cliff's carving is CPU geometry, so the GLB is the same surface, and the gold seam still runs live from its uniforms. If the CD prefers it code-native, the lightmap can be loaded on its own onto the kit mesh. | `assets.html`, `js/kit/strata.js` |
| P7-5 | **Match gate:** each packed GLB is rendered beside its kit in the same camera and light, AO off; pass when the mean difference is ≤ 1% and no more than 2% of pixels move by more than 16 of 255, in every view. Triangles may fall up to 0.5% under the kit (Draco drops degenerate triangles), never rise. | §7.1 asks for a turntable compared to the style frame. The kits render the style frames, so kit-versus-GLB is that comparison made exact; 2% follows the §11.1 visual-suite threshold. | `assets.html` (MATCH) |
| P7-6 | **Desktop downloads the High city fill, mobile the Mid**, and the decoders (Draco, Basis) count against both §7.2 3D budgets. | §10.1 puts phones on Mid. A visitor downloads the decoders too, so leaving them out would flatter the number. | `tools/pack.mjs` (SETS) |
| P7-7 | **Texture memory is an estimate**: KTX2 textures at 1 byte per pixel (BC7, ASTC 4×4 and ETC2 RGBA all are), uncompressed at 4, plus a third for mips. | WebGL does not report GPU memory. The estimate is conservative for ETC1S colour on devices that transcode to ETC1 or BC1 (half a byte per pixel). | `assets.html` (texBytes) |
| P7-8 | **The static props are modelled in code, not Blender.** The seal, specimens, city blocks and fault building are the G3 kits exported as GLB; Blender runs headless in CI for the AO bake only. | §7.1 step 1 says "model in Blender". The kits were signed at G2 and G3 as the look; remodelling them by hand would make a second source that has to be matched back to the frames. Exporting the kits keeps one source and makes the match gate (P7-5) exact. | `assets.html`, `tools/bake.py` |
